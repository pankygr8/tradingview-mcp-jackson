Intraday ↓Swing ↓

How to read it. R = profit per trade in units of the amount risked (entry − stop). Exp R = average R per trade after costs. Random R = what the same stops and targets earn with random entry times — a setup only has an edge if it beats this. Robust edge = profitable in both halves of the test, at least 30 trades, t ≥ 2.5 (computed on per-day / per-week averages, so fifty stocks moving together on one day count once), and at least 0.10R better than random entries. *Qty = shares for a ₹1,000 risk between entry and stop.
