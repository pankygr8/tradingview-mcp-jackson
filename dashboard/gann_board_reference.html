Gann Ladder Board

Intraday desk reference · NSE F&O · Square-of-9 levels off the session open
Every number here is structure or coincident state — opening range, relative volume, range expansion, Gann rungs. Nothing is smoothed. Nothing lags.

01The one thing to understand

A setup is not a permanent property of a stock. It decays through the tabs as the session ages. The same name is Actionable at 9:35, Late by 10:30, and Missed by noon — not because it got worse, but because the level it was sitting on is now behind it. This is the whole board in one picture.

Two independent clocks close the window. Extension — price walks away from its opening-range break, so the level you would have entered on is no longer there. Feasibility — the session shortens, so there is less time for price to travel to TP1. A name can fail on either. By noon most fail on both.

02What each tab holds

Trade from one tab. The rest exist so you can see what was filtered out, and why.

TabContainsWhat it's for
Actionable Passed all four gates The only tab you place orders from. Empty is a valid answer — it means no stock is sitting on a clean break with time left to reach its target.
Top movers Everything, ranked, ungated The day's character. Includes churn and spent moves. Read it to understand the tape, not to trade it.
Late 0.35–1.2 OR-widths past the break You're entering mid-move: the stop is further away and part of the reward is already gone. Reduced size, or skip.
Missed > 1.2 OR-widths extended The move happened. Only useful as a watchlist for a retest back to the opening range.
Churn Range expansion < 1.0× High volume, no range — heavy two-way trade going nowhere. The classic intraday trap: volume is not direction. These would top the board if score were additive.
Confluence On a rung, inside a time window Price within 0.25% of a Square-of-9 level while the clock sits in a Gann reversal window. Flags where a reaction is likely — not which direction. Context, not a signal.

03Reading a card

The ladder is a price axis. Everything on it was computable at 9:30 — before the bars that follow.

1Square-of-9 rungs — computed from the day's open the moment it prints. Spacing adapts to each stock's own median daily range, so one rung ≈ one typical day's travel divided six ways.
2SL · TP1 · TP2 — stop sits beyond the opening-range boundary, then targets are 1R and 2R snapped up to the next rung, so every exit lands on a Gann level.
3Price marker — the arrow riding the rail. When it comes within 0.25% of a rung, that rung thickens and pulses.
4Gann 50% line — the midpoint of yesterday's range. Gann's single most important intraday decision level.
5Opening-range band — 9:15–9:30 high and low. Direction comes from which side price is on; nothing else intraday is both structural and available this early.
6PDH / PDL — yesterday's high and low. Static, known before the bell, and where the day's decisions tend to happen.

04The four gates into Actionable

A name must clear all four. Each one exists because a specific real setup fooled the screener first.

Range is expanding

range_expansion ≥ 1.0× — today's range beats its own average. HDFCLIFE traded 7.27× normal volume on 0.80× range and ranked second on the board. Volume without range is a fight, not a trend.

The level is still there

extension_or ≤ 0.35 — price hasn't walked away from its break. DR REDDY was 6.48 OR-widths gone by the time the card rendered.

Time to reach TP1

tp1_feasible — expected remaining travel is ADR × √(session left). If TP1 is further than 70% of that, the trade needs a move the day no longer has room for.

A sane stop

0.15% ≤ risk ≤ 0.6 × ADR — tighter than 0.15% is inside the noise and the round-trip cost. Wider is not an intraday risk profile.

05What this board cannot do

  • The price is roughly 15 minutes old. Yahoo's intraday feed for .NS symbols is delayed. Every card shows stale_minutes. Use the board to pick the name and the level; take the fill on live data through TradingView.
  • R:R at TP1 is ≈1.0 by construction. TP1 is one unit of risk, so that ratio is a design choice, not evidence of edge. The gate that actually rejects trades is feasibility. Read rr_tp2 if you want a number with content.
  • The Square-of-9 grid does not predict. It generates levels ahead of price, which is why it can be traded without lag. That is a real advantage over any smoothed indicator, and it is not the same thing as knowing where price will go.
  • Score weights are reasoned, not backtested. They rank a shortlist. They do not size a position, and they have never seen your fills.
